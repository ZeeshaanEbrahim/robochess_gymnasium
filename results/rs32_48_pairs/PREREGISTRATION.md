# RS32: PPO (D recipe) on 48 start-target pairs, 6 seeds, 5M steps (pre-registered before any run)
Question: can PPO with the D recipe learn the D29 place task from 48 start-target pairs, 30 neighbour moves and 18 longer moves (2 or 3 squares)?
Training pairs (48, uniform per episode, in this order): c2:c3 c3:c4 c4:c5 c5:c6 c6:c7 c7:c8 d2:d3 d3:d4 d4:d5 d5:d6 d6:d7 d7:d8 e2:e3 e3:e4 e4:e5 e5:e6 e6:e7 e7:e8 f2:f3 f3:f4 f4:f5 f5:f6 f6:f7 f7:f8 c4:d4 f5:e5 d3:e4 e6:d7 e5:e4 c6:c5 b2:b4 g3:g6 d7:d4 a5:c5 h4:e4 b6:e6 a3:c5 c3:f6 h3:f5 g7:d4 b7:d5 e2:g4 a3:d5 h6:e4 c2:e5 f3:d6 b4:e6 g6:e3
Replacements: none (48 of 48 pairs passed the expert gate, steps 130-180; results/rs32_48_pairs/expert_gate/TABLE.md)
Recipe (D): ppo_ent_coef 0.01, n_envs 4, linear lr decay 3e-4 to 0, ppo_mean_squash, D29 reward, SB3 defaults otherwise. 300-step episodes, 5,000,000 steps (final model at 5,005,312 steps: 611 rollouts of 8192). Seeds 0, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500. No other change to the learner.
Evaluation (fixed): final model only. scripts/eval_probe.py --pairs <the 48 pairs in the order above> --episodes 2400 (50 per pair), stochastic, eval seed 20000, torch-seeded, frozen VecNormalize (obs_rms and ret_rms identical before and after). Placement = env is_success.
Decision rules (fixed; thresholds not changed after results):
1. A seed learns if pooled placement over the 48 pairs is >= 50% AND placement is >= 20% (10 of 50) on EVERY one of the 48 pairs.
2. Result = number of seeds that learn, out of 6.
3. The claim "PPO with the D recipe learns 48 mixed pairs" holds if at least 4 of 6 seeds learn. Otherwise the claim is not achieved; document it, report which subset (neighbour 30 or longer 18) falls short; the RS31 24-pair result remains the multi-start claim.
4. Target tier, reported: pooled placement >= 80% per seed.
Reported, not gated: per-pair placement with Wilson 95% intervals; pooled placement for the 30 neighbour pairs, the 18 longer pairs and the 24 RS31 pairs separately, per seed, with Wilson intervals; number of pairs under 20%; policy std at 1M, 2M, 3M, 4M and the 5,000,000 checkpoint; ever_held per 10% chunk of training (Monitor CSVs; D pooled over the four files); training-time rolling placement per 100k steps; zero-shot held-out: (a) 12 forward pairs on files b and g: b2:b3 b3:b4 b4:b5 b5:b6 b6:b7 b7:b8 g2:g3 g3:g4 g4:g5 g5:g6 g6:g7 g7:g8 (25 per pair, 300 episodes); (b) the 19 RS29 pairs that are not training pairs (300 episodes), including the corner-to-corner moves, which stay untrained; deterministic vs stochastic placement on the 48 pairs (2 episodes per pair); eval vs training cross-check.
