## 1. Frozen protocol (quoted verbatim from `results/rs30_four_pairs/PREREGISTRATION.md`)

> ## Evaluation (fixed)
> - Final model only. scripts/eval_probe.py --pairs e4:e5,d4:d5,f4:f5,e3:e4,
>   --episodes 300 (75 per pair), stochastic, eval seed 20000, torch-seeded, frozen
>   VecNormalize. Placement = env is_success.
>
> ## Decision rules (fixed; thresholds not changed after results)
> 1. A seed learns four pairs if its placement rate is >= 20% on EACH of the 4 training
>    pairs (75 episodes each).
> 2. An arm passes if >= 3 of its 4 seeds learn.
> 3. Interpretation fixed in advance:
>    - A passes: several start squares are learnable with the D29 config.
>    - A fails, B passes: the entropy bonus is needed at this budget; it becomes the
>      candidate for any wider follow-up.
>    - Both fail: learning more than one start square is not achieved at this budget;
>      the generalisation track stops and is documented.
>    - Both pass: the entropy bonus is not necessary at four pairs.
> 4. D30 (fixed-move T1 benchmark) stands unless a change is decided before 12 Oct.
>
> ## Reported, not gated
> - Per-pair placement with Wilson 95% intervals, per seed and arm.
> - Held-out near pairs d5:d6 and c4:c5 (zero-shot, 75 episodes each) and the 20 RS29
>   pairs (zero-shot, 300 episodes), same protocol.
> - Policy std (exp log_std) at 100k, 300k, 500k, 1M, 2M per seed; Monitor ever_held
>   per 10% chunk.
> - e4:e5 placement vs D29 squash (92.3, 0, 96.0, 99.3, 97.3).

## 6. Definitions and known artefact

- `ever_held` (training Monitor): the env latch; True once any step had both pads on the piece and no piece-board contact.
- `place.held` (eval JSON): the same condition (both pads on the piece, no piece-board contact), true if any step satisfied it; no lift or closure needed.
- `grasp_ever` (eval JSON): any step with `info['grasp_success']` (`_grasp_success`): the same pad and no-board-contact condition, and additionally the piece origin more than 10 mm above the board and the gripper closed.
- Placement = env `is_success` on the last step (`place.placed`).
- **Known artefact:** the `lifted_30mm` and `carried_within_3cm` funnel columns read 0 even for the 99.3% control (control above: held 300/300, lifted_30mm 0/300, carried_within_3cm 0/300, released 298/300, placed 298/300). They are not used as funnel stages anywhere in this directory.

## 7. Deviations

- From `PREREGISTRATION.md`: none. The evaluation used the final model only, `--pairs e4:e5,d4:d5,f4:f5,e3:e4`, 300 episodes (75 per pair), stochastic, seed 20000, frozen VecNormalize, thresholds unchanged.
- The held-out episode count was 75 per pair for d5:d6 and c4:c5 (150 episodes), as in the pre-registration file; 300 episodes (15 per pair) on the 20 RS29 pairs.
- The tooling dry run used 8 episodes (2 per pair), run twice with identical outputs, because `eval_probe.py` requires the episode count to be a multiple of the pair count and 10 is not divisible by 4.
- `scripts/eval_probe.py` was not edited. Because its JSON carries no git SHA, the SHA and clean-tree status are recorded in `EVAL_MANIFEST.json` instead.
- The `zeroshot` command in `run_evals.py` is new tooling (it only builds the item 4 command lines and calls the unmodified `eval_probe.py`).

## 8. Not concluded

These are leads from the numbers above, not findings. None was tested here.

- Policy std fell below 0.1 on all 4 dims by the 2M checkpoint in all 4 arm A seeds and in none of the 4 arm B seeds. Whether the std difference is what separates the arms is not tested; the arms differ only in `ppo_ent_coef`.
- A0 and A3 hold the piece in a large share of episodes (145/300 and 175/300) and almost never release or place; where the failure sits is not localised.
- In several seeds the Monitor ever_held rate falls late in training (for example A0 chunks 7 to 9, A1 chunks 8 to 10); no cause was examined.
- Arm B zero-shot placement on d5:d6 and c4:c5 differs a lot by seed (for example B0 53/75 on d5:d6 and 0/75 on c4:c5; B3 11/75 and 59/75). Why is not examined.
- Zero-shot placements on the 20 RS29 pairs are on e4:e5 (a training pair) and, for 3 models, d4:e5; none on the mid, long or diagonal bands.
- 4 seeds per arm, one untuned `ppo_ent_coef` value, and all training moves one rank forward (caveats in the pre-registration).
